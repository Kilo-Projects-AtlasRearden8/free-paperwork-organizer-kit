# Free Paperwork Organizer Kit

Fillable paperwork organizers — a bill tracker, an appointment log, a form-filling cheat sheet, and a document checklist. Printable, large-print friendly, and no account needed.

His book *The Massive AI Manual* and hub site dylanasdale.github.io explain AI "like a friend would": plain English, step by step, never condescending.

## What's inside

| Organizer | What it helps with |
|---|---|
| **Bill Tracker** (`bill-tracker.html`) | Track bills, due dates, amounts, payment methods, and confirmation numbers month by month. |
| **Appointment Log** (`appointment-log.html`) | Log appointments with provider, reason, phone, address, what to bring, and whether each is confirmed. |
| **Form-Filling Cheat Sheet** (`form-filling-cheat-sheet.html`) | Fill in your personal info once, then copy it onto forms as needed — plus quick reminders so nothing gets missed. |
| **Document Checklist** (`document-checklist.html`) | Check off 24 important documents (ID, medical, financial, housing, legal, auto) and record where originals and copies are kept. |

Each organizer is available in two formats:

- **HTML** — open in any web browser, type into the shaded fields on screen, then print.
- **Markdown** (`.md`) — plain-text mirror you can read, print, or copy into any notes app.

## How to use

1. Download or open any `.html` file in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari). No account, no install, no internet needed after you have the file.
2. Click into the shaded fields and type, or print first and write by hand.
3. Print (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P). Turn on "Print background graphics" so the headers show. Pages are US Letter with large-print text and big checkboxes.
4. Or open a `.md` file in any Markdown reader or text editor.

## Designed to be friendly and accessible

- **Large-print friendly:** 16–17 px base text, generous row heights, and 22 px checkboxes that are easy to tap or mark.
- **High contrast:** dark text on a light background with dark-blue headers.
- **Local-first:** everything runs in your browser. Nothing is collected, stored, or sent anywhere — no account, no tracking.
- **ADHD-friendly:** one page per task, clear checkboxes, and short reminders so you can pick it up and put it down without losing your place.
- **Education only:** the form-filling cheat sheet and document checklist are not medical or legal advice.

## Safety reminder

Never carry your full Social Security number on paper. On the form-filling cheat sheet, write only the last 4 digits, and never email or text your full SSN, card numbers, or passwords. Store any sheet with personal information somewhere safe.
