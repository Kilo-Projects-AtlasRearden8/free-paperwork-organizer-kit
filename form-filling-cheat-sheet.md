## Quick reminders

- [ ] Use black or blue ink when writing by hand.
- [ ] Don't sign blank forms — fill in every field first, or write N/A.
- [ ] Keep a copy of everything you sign.
- [ ] Store this sheet somewhere safe; it has personal information on it.
- [ ] Ask for help if a form is confusing — clinic staff, librarians, and trusted friends can walk through it with you.
