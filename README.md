# Free Paperwork Organizer Kit

Fillable paperwork organizers — a bill tracker, an appointment log, a form-filling cheat sheet, and a document checklist. Printable, large-print friendly, no account needed.

Made by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** — beginner-friendly AI education, books and guides, and practical tools from Dylan Asdale (registered nurse turned AI author and founder of Asdale Dynamics, LLC, Indiana, formed October 2026). His book *The Massive AI Manual* and hub site dylanasdale.github.io explain AI "like a friend would": plain English, step by step, never condescending.

## What's inside

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `bill-tracker.html` / `bill-tracker.md` | Track bills, due dates, amounts, and payment confirmations month by month. |
| `appointment-log.html` / `appointment-log.md` | Log appointments with provider, reason, phone, address, what to bring, and confirmation status. |
| `form-filling-cheat-sheet.html` / `form-filling-cheat-sheet.md` | Fill in your personal info once, copy it onto forms as needed, plus quick form-filling reminders. |
| `document-checklist.html` / `document-checklist.md` | Check off 24 important documents and record where originals and copies are kept. |

## How to use

1. Open any `.html` file in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari — no account, no install, no internet needed after download).
2. Click into the shaded fields and type, or print first and write by hand.
3. Print (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P). Choose "Print background graphics" so headers show. The pages are US Letter with large-print text and big checkboxes.
4. Or read/print the `.md` versions in any Markdown reader.

## Design notes

- Large-print friendly: 16–17 px base text, generous row heights, 22 px checkboxes.
- High contrast, US Letter layout, tested for clean printing.
- Local-first: everything runs in your browser. Nothing is collected, stored, or sent anywhere.
- Education only. The form-filling cheat sheet and document checklist are not medical or legal advice.
